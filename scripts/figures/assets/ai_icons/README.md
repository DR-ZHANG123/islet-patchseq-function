# Explanatory icons for Fig. 1

These PNG icons (transparent background) were generated once with GPT-Image-2 (OpenAI) on 2026-10-25 from the text
prompts listed in `PROMPTS.tsv`, using `generate_icon.py` (the endpoint and key are read from the environment variables
`IMAGE_API_BASE` and `IMAGE_API_KEY` and are never stored). They were cropped to their content and are embedded unchanged
by `scripts/figures/Fig1_framework.py`; rebuilding the figure does not call the image service. The figure caption and the
manuscript's AI declaration state their use. They are used only in explanatory schematics, not in data figures or the
graphical abstract.
